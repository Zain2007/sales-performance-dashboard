# Sales Performance Analysis Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project analyses a three-year sales dataset using **Microsoft
Excel** to identify sales performance by market, quarter, category, customer, model, business segment, color, and region. Overall performance is strong, with Total Sales of 81.0M across 60,919 orders and 214,516 units.

## Business Objectives

### The main objectives of this analysis are to:

- Monitor overall sales performance.
- Analyse sales trends across different quarters and markets.
- Identify top-performing product categories and models.
- Understand customer and regional sales performance.
- Analyse sales distribution by business segment and product colour.
- Identify opportunities for improving sales and business growth.

## Dashboard Overview
The dashboard offers key views including sales performance by market, quarterly sales analysis, top categories, top customers (overall and by sales), top models, sales by business segment, sales by colour, and sales by region, all supported by interactive filters for year, region, subregion, and colour.

|---|---|

## Recommendations
#### Focus on North America and high-performing products
- Strengthen the North American market while continuing to invest in Road Bikes and Mountain Bikes, the key revenue drivers.
#### Optimize Q3 inventory and promotions
- Prepare inventory and targeted marketing campaigns before Q3 to capture seasonal demand, especially for high-performing products and colours such as Black.
#### Diversify and improve underperforming areas
- Expand the Components segment while targeting lower-performing customers, products, and markets with personalized campaigns and performance improvements.

## Conclusion
The dashboard reveals strong, concentrated sales performance, primarily driven by the Bikes segment, North America, leading product categories, and high-value customers. Future growth lies in expanding weaker markets, developing the Components segment, improving customer engagement, and optimizing lower-performing products.
